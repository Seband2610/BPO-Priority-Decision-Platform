Priority Classification Guide

Understand how the platform evaluates and classifies customer requests.

CRITICAL | Score 9+

Immediate Attention

Highest priority. Very high urgency, critical SLA conditions or premium customer impact can increase the score.

HIGH | Score 7–8

High Priority

Requires priority handling and close operational monitoring.

MEDIUM | Score 4–6

Moderate Priority

Requires normal processing and appropriate follow-up.

LOW | Score 0–3

Low Priority

Can follow the regular operational workflow.

How is the Priority Score Calculated?

Urgency:

  • Urgency 9–10: +5 points
  • Urgency 7–8: +3 points
  • Urgency 4–6: +2 points
  • Urgency 1–3: +1 point

SLA (Service Level Agreement):

  • 2 hours or less: +4 points
  • More than 2 and up to 4 hours: +3 points
  • More than 4 and up to 8 hours: +2 points
  • More than 8 hours: +0 points

Customer Tier:

  • Premium customer: +2 points
  • Standard customer: +0 points

Final score: Urgency points + SLA points + Customer Tier points.

The platform calculates the score automatically and assigns the corresponding priority level.
